Feb 9, 2026 ·AkerSolutionshas reported revenues of Nkr16.7bn ($1.7bn) for the fourth quarter (Q4) of 2025, representing a 6% increase from Nkr15.7bn in the same period of 2024. Full-year revenues reached Nkr63.2bn, marking a 19% year-on-year growth from Nkr53.2bn in 2024.
